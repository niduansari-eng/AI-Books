PeerBooks MVP (Version 1.0)

Objective

Launch a cloud-based accounting platform that allows SMEs to manage their accounting records without relying on spreadsheets or multiple disconnected systems.

Target Customers

- Trading businesses
- Manufacturing businesses
- Service businesses
- Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs)

Excluded from Version 1.0:

- Advanced textile export workflows
- Export documentation management
- Letter of Credit management
- Industry-specific export compliance modules

Mandatory Modules

Authentication

- User Login
- User Logout
- Password Reset
- User Roles

Company Management

- Company Registration
- Company Profile
- User Assignment

Accounting

- Chart of Accounts
- Journal Entries
- General Ledger
- Trial Balance

Sales

- Customers
- Sales Invoices
- Sales Reporting

Purchases

- Vendors
- Purchase Invoices
- Purchase Reporting

Expenses

- Expense Recording
- Expense Categories

Inventory

- Product Master
- Inventory Tracking
- Stock Valuation

Financial Reports

- Trial Balance
- Profit and Loss Statement
- Balance Sheet

Success Criteria

Version 1.0 is successful when a company can maintain complete accounting records and generate financial statements without using external accounting software.

Product Philosophy

Strong accounting foundation first.

AI features will be introduced only after core accounting functions are reliable and production-ready.
